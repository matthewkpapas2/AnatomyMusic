## 3. Fake-out drop (#39), PR `fix-fakeout-hole`
**Diagnosis, by rendering the previews and reading their level every 100 ms:**

| Pair | What the "dead air" actually was |
|---|---|
| Demo 1 → Demo 2 | Demo 1's build ends in a one-beat silence. The echo was sent that silent beat, so the fake bar sat at about −105 dB. **The echo bug #39 described.** |
| Drugs From Amsterdam → Losing It / → Turn Off The Lights | **Not the echo bar** (it held at −1 to −12 dB). The preview opened inside Drugs From Amsterdam's own fade to −36 dB at 1:31, because the detected build starts in a breakdown. |

The other 14 Fake-outs had 0–0.6 s of dead air. Echo feedback 0.5 still fades about 24 dB over the bar (the demo's last
0.9 s sat at −20 to −29 dB even with a sounding beat).

**Change:** the echo takes the last beat with sound in it (a beat more than 10 dB under the loudest of the three before
it counts as silent), and Track 1's fader comes down where the silence starts. The lead-in skips bars with any beat 20 dB
under the song's median mids/highs (at least 2 bars kept). With matched tempos, a second version plays Track 2's last
build bar (bass off) under the echoes. That version brings Track 2 in a bar early, and **as the only version it lost 4
Fake-outs to the one-voice rule** (Levels ↔ Animals, Losing It → girl$, → Drugs From Amsterdam). So both versions are
built and the sound check picks: the demo takes the fill, the 4 pairs keep the echo.

|---|---|---|
| Drugs From Amsterdam → Losing It | 48 (2.25 s dead air, paper 76) | **100** (0.25 s, paper 96) |
| Drugs From Amsterdam → Turn Off The Lights | 48 (2.25 s, paper 77) | **100** (0.25 s, paper 96) |

No other idea's score changed in any of the 24 directions. One Fake-out moved at the same score: Nanana → Where You

**Said plainly:** on Drugs From Amsterdam the 48 → 100 is mostly the preview starting later, after the song's own fade.
The DJ's steps still say to let the build run from its start (1:31 included), so for the DJ nothing changed on that

This was the first intentional `web/` change, so the byte-identity check retired (last passed at `e5a8f9d`).

## 4. Gap-only landings (#31), PR `fix-gap-landings`
**Measured on Track 2's own audio at all 39 gap-only entries in the 20 songs** (36 in songs that are a Track 2 somewhere) (bass over the 4 bars after vs. before):
- The worst landing went **into Nanana's breakdown** at 2:28 (bass then falls 31 dB): the Silence switch that scored 20
  and landed 6.7 LU quieter (10-08 finding 8).
- Animals 1:07 (the landing for 6 of Rumble → Animals' ideas; a 7th used 2:07) is a mid-section stop: after 0.93 s of silence the music
  carries on with less bass than before. Latch 0:05 is the intro (mids −22 dB).
- Three "earn it" rules were tried (bass up ≥ 3 dB; not into a breakdown; plus "not within 8 bars of a confident drop").
  Each either passed only same-level mid-section stops (+0 to +1 dB) or rejected the one useful entry (Pipe Down 0:12,
  where the beat comes in, +7.8 dB). **So gap-only entries stop being landings.**
- Stopping alone took **Kelly → Pipe Down from 12 ideas to 3**: Pipe Down's only drop is a low-confidence 0:06, and the
  chorus/peak fallback only ran when there were no landings at all. It now also runs when Track 2 has no confident
  (≥ 0.6) drop or hit, and skips section starts before 0.5 s (a landing at 0:00 is just "start the song").

|---|---|---|
| Beginner | 140 · 96.1 (min 0) | 137 · 96.2 (min 0) |

**What it cost (12 ideas):** Rumble → Animals loses all 5 Intermediate ideas (73–100: four on Animals 1:07, a Shared-chord

Compatibility rose on Where You Are → Nanana (82 → 85).

## 5. Pausing Visuals (#35), PR `harness-no-raf-throttle`
**Misdiagnosed on 10-08; no app change.** Main-thread CPU in headless Chromium, 10 s after the page settles:

| Page | CPU |
|---|---|
| Visuals | ~103% (software WebGL; the same with the rAF throttle on) |
| Visuals → switched to Mix | 4.1% |
| Mix with two songs loaded | 8.0% (1.5% throttled) |
| Visuals → switched to Analysis | 42.6% (Analysis's own spectrogram and lanes) |

`loop()` only calls `VZ.frame` while Visuals is showing, and browsers pause `requestAnimationFrame` in background tabs.
The "half a CPU" came from the harness, which opened on Visuals. It now opens `?debug#mix` and no longer patches rAF.
**Same ideas, cues, scores and compatibility in all 24 directions** (raw measurements differ by about 1e-5 dB, the
renderer's noise). While measuring, a harness bug crashed a whole run (a stuck file chooser left a promise rejecting with
nothing listening): fixed in the same PR.

## 7. What this says about the docs' 10-08 diagnoses
Two of the four fixes asked for this session rested on a misread: Drugs From Amsterdam's Fake-out hole was the song, not
the echo, and the Visuals cost was the harness. Both were only visible by rendering and timing the real thing, which is
the argument for keeping "measure before changing" as a rule, not a formality.
