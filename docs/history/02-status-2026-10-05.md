## v7: what changed and why
**Diagnosis (measured on the v6 baseline).** Every preview was rendered headlessly, mixed and with each deck solo, for Kelly→Bieber, John→Bieber, Kelly→Drake and the demo pair:
1. **The in-browser stem split was the root cause of "higher levels sound worse".**
   - The "no vocals" stem lowered the singer by only 4–6 dB.
   - The "vocals only" stem kept the hats and snare at only −4 to −6 dB. It was a full mix minus bass.
   - So Acapella mash-in and Vocal carry-over stacked two singers. They measured 0–37 out of 100, with 4–14 s of vocal clash per preview.
   - Intermediate averaged 74 (minimum 3) and Advanced 77 (minimum 10), against Beginner's 95.
2. **v6 couldn't see drops.**
   - It relied on the engine's moments (a silence gap or a detected riser), so it found **no drops** in John Summit's house track, which has four.
   - Most transitions landed Track 2 mid-section ("section D") after long overlaps.
   - The median bass jump at the moment Track 2's bass arrives was **0.5 dB**. That is the "clunky fade".
3. Trade phrases chopped singers mid-line, and only Borrowed drop and Drop hijack targeted a drop at all.

**Fixes (AnatomyMix, Node-testable):**
- **`findDrops(P)`, a drop detector working bar by bar from the audio**, with two shapes:
  - **Bass return:** the kick band (<130 Hz) and kick onsets come back. A full drop is ≥ 9 dB over 4 bars; a pop chorus hit is ≥ 6 dB after a one-bar stop.
  - **Riser drop:** the highs climbed ≥ 8 dB over 4 bars and then fall back, while the low end and mids get fuller.
  - A bass return that only starts a riser build is treated as the build's start.
  - Each drop records its build (start and length, plus whether a riser or snare roll was present), sustain, vocals at the drop and confidence. The engine's silence gaps are attached as `gap`.
  - A confident drop **moves a section boundary within 2 bars onto itself**. Kelly's chorus boundaries were 2 bars early.
  - The demos match the known answers exactly (1:03.0; 0:38.1 and 1:16.2). John gets 0:17, 1:03, 1:33 and 3:05. Kelly gets 0:44 and 1:32 (hits) and 2:12 (a drop). Bieber gets 0:08 (a drop) and 1:00 and 2:15 (hits).
- **Every idea is built from a landing and a launch.**
  - **Landing:** one of Track 2's drops or hits (`landings(C)`), with chorus starts as a fallback, preferring early ones.
  - **Launch:** where Track 1 leaves (`launches(C)`): the end of its own build-up (`build`) or a phrase line (`phrase`).
  - Each idea carries `land` (the plan time), `landing`, `tension` and `special` (the "why it's special" line).
- **New and rebuilt ideas (22 generators):**
  - **Beginner:** Blend into the drop, Filter riser, Cut to the drop, Echo into the drop, Tape stop.
  - **Intermediate:** Drop swap, Double drop, Loop roll, Spinback, Wash-out (reverb), Key lift, Loop & layer, Shared-chord drop, Half-time drop, Speed ramp.
  - **Advanced:** Fake-out drop, Build into build, Silence switch, Wormhole (phrase lines only), Harmonic key change, Key-lock-off blend, Tempo ride, Trade phrases.
  - **Removed:** Acapella mash-in, Vocal carry-over and Filter swap. The stem splitter (`separate`) was deleted from Mix and App, not left dormant.
- **Bass kill before the drop.** Cut, Drop swap, Key lift, Roll, Spinback and Wash-out all turn Track 1's bass down for the last beat or bar, as one EQ move in the steps.
- **Pickups up to a full bar** (v6 stopped at 2 beats). During a pickup, Track 1's mids step aside (`landB(…, a)`).
- **"Cut mid-line" now asks whether the singer would have kept singing after the cut.** v6's ¾-beat-before rule missed lines that run straight across the phrase line. A voice that ends inside an exit effect (echo, reverb, roll, brake, spinback) costs only 30%.
- **Sound check: 6 rows.**
  - Rows: rhythm .27, phrase .15, voice .25, key .12, **flow .13**, **clean .08**. `total = 0.75·weighted + 0.25·worst`.
  - **Flow** comes from `planLevels()`, a band-power model of the plan (each song's measured eLo/eMid/eHi × fader, isolator and filters, plus echo and reverb tails). It penalizes holes outside the pre-drop build zone and anticlimaxes (landing ≥ 4 dB quieter).
  - **Clean** penalizes key-locked stretch > 6% and shifts ≥ 2 semitones.
  - The check also reports `contrast`: the low-band jump at the landing.
- **Payoff and ranking.**
  - `payoff = 0.4·landing value (kind, impact, early) + 0.25·tension + 0.35·contrast/12`.
  - `score = 0.55·check + 0.45·payoff`.
- **Sound floor:**
  - The check must be ≥ **70 / 72 / 75** at Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced.
  - The **one-voice row must be ≥ 0.45 at Intermediate and ≥ 0.6 at Advanced**.
  - Ideas that fail are listed under "Checked but not suggested" with their score and weakest row.
- **Compatibility (`compatibility(C, all)`, returned as `result.compat`):**
  - Weights: tempo .20, key .15, drops .18, loudness .05 (trim fixes it, so it costs little), vocals .07, best transition .35.
  - Labels: Great (≥ 85), Good (≥ 70), Workable (≥ 55), Tough.

**App and UI (the v6 design kept):**
- **The compatibility card at the top of the Mix tab:** a big number, a verdict word and 6 bars with one line each. It's hidden until both songs are analyzed.
- **Drop triangles on both deck waveforms** (bigger for full drops).
- **On the stage:** a ▼ marks where the drop lands, and a reverb tail is drawn. A "Why it's special" line (with payoff and sound scores) sits above a **6-part sound check** (3×2 on desktop, 2 columns ≤ 1080 px, 1 column ≤ 720 px).
- **Previews:** a **reverb send** with a ConvolverNode on a generated, seeded impulse (decaying stereo noise that darkens), fed before the fader so the tail rings after the cut. No impulse file, no dependency.
- **Copy:** the level notes, glossary (added "Build-up, drop" and "Echo, reverb wash", removed "Stems, acapella") and "How the Mix tab decides" (Drops, the 6-row sound check, Ranking, Compatibility, Previews without stems) were rewritten.

## Known issues / tech debt
1. **No version control.** There are now seven numbered copies in Downloads. The platform move (below) should start with a repo.
2. **CSS layered overrides** in the header (from v3). The v7 Mix CSS adds a block and changes the `.check` grid in place, with no overrides and no `!important`.
3. **Analysis page dead space** under Spectrum.
4. **Detection lag in the engine.** The mixer corrects its own sections; the Analysis tab still shows the engine's.
5. `f0F`/`trackNote` naming is a v2 holdover.
6. **The mood estimate is crude.**
7. **File input only exists on the Mix tab.**
8. Sessions and Mix decks are capped at 600 s.
9. **Engine timestamps are early/late.** Chords still use `CHORD_LAG`.
10. **Mix analysis runs on the main thread.**
11. **Section roles and local keys are heuristics.**
12. **Previews are approximations** (WSOLA key lock; tempo ride and speed ramp run without key lock).
13. **4 script blocks.**
14. **One `localStorage` key** (`anatomy.mix.level`).
15. **The vocal detector isn't a true vocal detector.** It looks for centered, pitched, syllabic sound, so it can't work on mono files. This matters more in v7: the one-voice floors are what thin out Advanced on vocal-heavy pop.
16. ~~In-browser separation is rough~~ **Resolved in v7 by removal.** If real stems come back, it should be an ML model in the platform phase.
17. **Two FFTs** (`AnatomyMix.fftC` duplicates the engine's).
18. ~~Vocal ideas accept stretch tempo gaps~~ Gone with the vocal ideas. The Clean row now penalizes key-locked stretch > 6%.
19. **Two rhythm analyses** (engine vs mixer grid).
20. **A second loudness measure** (LUFS vs the engine's RMS).
21. **The sound-check, payoff, ranking and compatibility weights are judgment calls.** In v7 that covers rows .27/.15/.25/.12/.13/.08, `score = .55 check + .45 payoff`, and the compatibility weights. **This is exactly what the dataset phase should fit.**
22. **The phrase dynamic program's weights** were tuned on the demos plus 4 songs.
23. **The limiter is JS post-processing.**
24. **NEW (v7): the drop detector's thresholds** (9 dB / 4 bars, 6 dB after a 1-bar stop, riser +8/−4 dB) were tuned on the 2 demos and 4 songs. They should be validated against Raveform's expert labels.
25. **NEW (v7): `planLevels()` is a power model on paper** (band energies × gains). It has no phase or masking, and the filter roll-off is approximate. The rendered harness is the real check.
26. **NEW (v7): the measured harness and the paper check still disagree sometimes.** The harness counts any word still being sung when a clip ends as a cut, even under an echo tail, while the paper check discounts effect exits to 30%. Fake-out and Silence switch on vocal pop measure lower than their paper scores.
27. **NEW (v7): Advanced is thin on vocal-heavy pop** (2 ideas per real pair). That's because of the one-voice minimum (0.6), which is intentional. House tracks with instrumental breakdowns should do better. This is untested until we have more house test songs.
28. **NEW (v7): `AnatomyMix._internal.gens()`** exposes the generator table for the Node test harness (`why.js`). It's debug-only, documented here.
29. **NEW (v7): the lyrics module is drafted but not shipped** (`lyr.js` in the v7 work folder: parsing, alignment, matching). It's parked by Matt's decision. Nothing lyric-related is in anatomy7.html.

## Decisions (2026-10-05)
- **Lyrics: parked** until drops are solid.
  - Tested (2026-09-30): lrclib.net allows any origin, so a page opened as a local file in Chrome can fetch synced lyrics. All 4 test songs have LRC there.
  - Search by artist and title, then pick the closest duration (plain `/api/get` returned the wrong edit for Bieber).
  - Matt: target Chrome only, keep the HTML.
  - The container and the Mac's sandboxed shell can't reach lrclib, so real-song lyric tests need the browser.
- **The Spotify API is not usable for training or analysis:**
  - Spotify's developer policy bans training on its content.
  - The Audio Features and Audio Analysis endpoints have been closed to new apps since Nov 27, 2024.
  - Playback audio is DRM-protected.
- **Direction (Matt):**
  - Emphasize drops.
  - Show a compatibility score at the top.
  - Use real DJ data to learn what makes a great mix. Uniformity in that data is fine ("there's a formula").
  - Offer a basic-to-very-creative range, possibly with the **Claude API** for the creative end.
  - Leaving the single HTML file is OK.
- **Order:** drop-first v7 in the HTML (done), **then the platform and data phase**. **Genre: house/EDM first** (that's where the data is).
- **Audio download for private research: approved by Matt.** It has to run in Matt's own Terminal: the cloud container and the Cowork VM are both blocked from YouTube, SoundCloud, MixesDB and Hugging Face.

## Next: platform + data phase (v8, not started)
**Datasets:**
- **Raveform** (TISMIR, April 2026, CC BY 4.0): 4,902 mixes, 53,780 transitions with mix points, and 1,423 tracks with expert labels (intro / buildup / drop / breakdown / outro, beats, downbeats). The annotations are on Hugging Face (`taejunkim/raveform`, `raveform.zip`), and the code repo is still empty.
- **EDM-CUE** (ETH 2024): 21,461 cue points from 4 professional DJs across 4,710 tracks. The code, model and dataset are public at `ETH-DISCO/cue-detr`.
- **DJ Mix Dataset** (Kim et al. 2020): 5,040 mixes of metadata in `mir-aidj/djmix-dataset` (`dataset/djmix-dataset.json`, 11.6 MB, already cloned in the v7 session), with alignment and EQ-estimation code. Audio comes via YouTube and SoundCloud links.

**Plan:**
1. Set up a repo for a local web app (Node server plus the current UI in Chrome) and a Python data pipeline.
2. Matt runs the download script in his own Terminal, starting with a subset: ~200–300 house/EDM mixes plus their tracks.
3. Validate `findDrops` against Raveform's labels.
4. Learn where DJs mix in and out relative to drops (from Raveform's mix points).
5. Fit the ranking, payoff and sound-check weights with a small pairwise ranker that predicts the real DJ's choice among Anatomy's candidates.
6. Add a **Claude API** "creativity dial" on the server. It sees the measured analysis, the candidate plans and retrieved real DJ transitions, and proposes or ranks creative variants. **The measured sound check keeps a veto**, and Claude can't hear audio.
7. Add a **rating control** on previews, so Matt's own taste becomes training data.
8. Later: real stems (an ML model) if wanted, and the lyric-matching layer.
