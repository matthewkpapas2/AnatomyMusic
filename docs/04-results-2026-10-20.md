## 1. #44: the payoff formula, written once (PR #7)
`finish()` and `rate()` each had a copy of the payoff formula, and the copies had already drifted in three ways that could
change a result, plus their guards:

| | `rate()` | `finish()` | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| No landing | the full formula with a landing value of 0.3 | the whole payoff replaced by `0.3 + 0.3·idea` | `finish()`'s |
| Tension 0 | became 0.5 (`\|\| 0.5`) | stayed 0 | `finish()`'s |
| NaN contrast | 0 | NaN (`clamp` passes NaN through) | 0 |
| Guard | landing present | landing present and `land` set | `finish()`'s |

(`rate()` also left its total unclamped, but both inputs are already in [0, 1], so that never mattered.)

None of these fire today (every `rate()` caller has been through `landed()`, and every tension is a positive constant).
Now there's one `payoffOf(x, contrast)` plus `rankOf(check, payoff)`.

**Measured:** all 338 ideas identical (rank, score, paper score, payoff, idea score, landing time). Compatibility and
the skipped lists are identical in all 24 directions.
